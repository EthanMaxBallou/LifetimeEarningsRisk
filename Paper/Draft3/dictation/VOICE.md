# Voice and conversion rules

Applied to every transcript → prose conversion.

## Conversion rules

1. **Arrange, don't compose.** Reorder, cut filler, merge fragments into
   sentences, supply connective tissue. Do not introduce claims, numbers,
   citations, or hedges that weren't said.
2. **Prefer his words.** When a phrasing works, keep it verbatim. Don't upgrade
   vocabulary for the sake of sounding academic — that's how voice leaks out.
3. **Glossary first.** Apply `GLOSSARY.md` mechanically before interpreting
   anything. Flag unrecognized garbled terms; never guess.
4. **Contradictions surface, don't resolve.** If he says two incompatible things,
   note it — don't silently pick one.
5. **Every conversion ships with a diff note:**
   - *Dropped* — things he said that didn't make the prose, and why
   - *Added* — anything in the prose he didn't say (should be near-empty; if it
     isn't, the conversion has drifted)
   - *Flagged* — garbled terms, unresolved contradictions, claims needing a
     number checked against the output files
6. **Numbers get verified**, never transcribed from memory or from speech. Pull
   from `OtherOutput/SecondDraft2/` and say which file.

## Voice characteristics

*To be filled from the first transcripts — sentence length, characteristic
constructions, how technical he goes unprompted, where he hedges and where he
doesn't, recurring phrasings worth preserving.*
